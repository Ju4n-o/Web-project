NRG Park Houston Rodeo

Event & Activity Info


Pig racing

One of the most fun attractions at the rodeo is the pig racing show. Small pigs race around a short track while announcers introduce them with funny names and stories. Kids and families cheer for their favorite pig as they run to the finish line. The races are fast, exciting, and usually happen several times during the day.

Traditional Round Up

The traditional roundup celebrates the cowboy history and culture of Texas. During a roundup, cowboys and cowgirls show skills that were originally used on cattle ranches. These activities can include roping, horseback riding, and cattle herding demonstrations. The roundup helps visitors understand the traditions of ranch life and why the rodeo is such an important part of Texas culture.

Fun Rides for all Ages

The rodeo carnival has many rides for visitors of all ages. There are gentle rides for younger children, exciting roller coasters for teens, and classic rides like Ferris wheels for families to enjoy together. The bright lights and music of the carnival create a fun atmosphere, especially at night. Many visitors spend hours exploring rides and playing carnival games.


Food Stands

The rodeo is famous for its wide variety of food. Visitors can find classic fair foods like corn dogs, funnel cakes, turkey legs, and cotton candy. There are also Texas favorites such as barbecue, tacos, and smoked brisket. Many food stands are run by local vendors, giving people a chance to try unique and delicious meals while enjoying the event.
