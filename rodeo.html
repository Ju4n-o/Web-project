Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo

The Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo promotes agriculture by hosting an annual, family-friendly experience that educates and entertains the public, supports Texas youth, showcases Western heritage and provides year-round educational support within the community

What is the Houston Livestock Rodeo

The Houston Livestock Rodeo is the worlds largest indoor livestock exhibitions, rodeo, and entertainment event, held in Houston, Texas. Holding and inviting nearly 2 million people across the span of 3 weeks. This event takes place from March 2-22 at NRG Park. The Houston Livestock Rodeo promotes agriculture and hosts family friendly experiences that educate and entertain the public. Supporting Texas youth through educational opportunities and many communities. Additionally, this event hosts concerts with echoes of big artists such as Beyonce, Taylor Swift, Elvis Presley, Roy Rogers, Cardi B, Drake, 50 Cent, George Strait, Alan Kaclson, and Charley Pride are just some of the many who have performed in the Houston Livestock Rodeo.

The Houston Livestock Rodeo has many diffirent activities and food stands to enjoy your time here. It showcases the agriculture scene and fives into diverse cultures From diffirent stations and exhbiits of farm expereinces to rides that make you eager to get on.
